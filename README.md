# MRI Signal to Image

Interactive teaching module on how MRI forms a brain image, for research study staff: magnetization, RF excitation, relaxation, spatial encoding, k-space, reconstruction, motion artifact, contrast and the study protocol.

Live: https://jfrascino.github.io/mri-signal-to-image/

Scans are from one research volunteer; hand-drawn overlays are labeled as illustrations. Simulations are qualitative. Not a medical resource.
